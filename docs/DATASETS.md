## Synthetic generation and labels

`labeled_dataset(seed=42)` creates 15 beacon channels at 30, 60, and 300 second nominal intervals and jitter fractions 0%, 10%, 20%, 30%, and 50%; three periodic benign channels; and three irregular browsing channels. The periodic benign cases are deliberately labeled benign even though their timing resembles beacons. Their four-host fan-in topology is synthetic metadata assembled by `synthetic_topology`, not inferred from packet addresses.

Use `python -m beaconscope.evaluate --synthetic --seed 42` to reproduce the report. Changing the seed changes random timestamps, payload sizes, and dropped observations. The current results are development metrics and must not be represented as held-out accuracy.

## External data

No public capture is currently part of this project or has been evaluated. Before adding a third-party PCAP, document the exact source URL, dataset/version, license or terms of use, file hash, label convention, and transformations. Do not tune on the held-out set later used for reported performance. RITA comparisons also require recording the exact RITA/Zeek versions and configuration.
